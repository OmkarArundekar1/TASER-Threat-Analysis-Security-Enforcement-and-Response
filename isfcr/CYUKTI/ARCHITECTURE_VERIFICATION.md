## Summary of real gaps found (not fixed this phase — documented, not hidden)

1. **Shuffle → MISP has no direct reference**, only a shared `campaign_id`. A `PlaybookExecution` never records the MISP event ID its `create_incident` action may correspond to.
2. **Investigation has no persisted Neo4j identity** — real, direct, in-memory connection to the campaign, but no historical record of "which investigations ran when."
3. **The two MISP decision mechanisms remain unwired**, by design, pending the conditions stated in `MISP_PUBLICATION_POLICY.md`.
4. **Campaign correlation's and severity's full per-feature decision breakdowns** are computed and returned but not durably persisted (only the final score/outcome is) — see `DECISION_AUDITABILITY.md`.
